Spiral Node is a pocket e-ink device that hands you ideas and leaves the thinking to you. Today its code, hardware notes and scripts are open source.

## What's inside

- **Spiral OS**, in Python. It runs on the device and, through Pyodide, right in your browser.
- **Scripts**: small loops that show something to think about. Fun Fact + Question, Quick Story and the new Connect come built in.
- **Hardware notes**: the prototype parts and the form factor. The hardware is a prototype in progress, and battery life isn't measured yet.

## Connect

Connect shows two random topics from different categories and asks one question: what links these? It never explains. The connection is yours to make.

## Write a script

A script is one Python file and a library of facts, stories or topics. Copy the template, follow the [contributing guide](https://github.com/trooper7652-commits/spiral-node/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md), and try it in the [simulator](/try). Good first issues are waiting for you.
