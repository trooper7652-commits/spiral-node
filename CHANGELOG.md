## v0.1.0 (2026-11-21)

- Added: Spiral OS with seven built-in scripts: Fun Fact + Question, Quick Story, Connect, Chess Puzzles (solved one move at a time), Poems (public domain), On This Day and Space Facts
- Added: picture pages, today() and single-file scripts for script writers
- Added: Back and Home buttons
- Added: the website and in-browser simulator, with a page and share image for every script
- Added: the Today page, the in-browser script editor with Submit, Script of the Month (starts January 2027), design explorations, the build log and this changelog
- Added: the pager design (prototype in progress) and open hardware notes
