# Form factor

Prototype design, may change. There is no enclosure CAD yet.

## Shape

* A landscape pager with soft river-stone edges, about the size of a small phone turned on its side.
* A sealed, one-piece-looking body, like a phone. The outside has no antenna, IR window, pin header, visible screws or bulges.
* Matte olive (the lead colour), brown or black, with a copper accent.

## Front

* The page-margin e-ink screen runs across the top, inside a wide paper border.
* Bottom right: the spiral wheel, kept subtle, with a thin copper ring and only 2–3 loops of groove. It gives up, down, left, right and a centre press.
* Bottom left: a Back key and a Home key side by side, small flat keys with an engraved left arrow and house. Next to them is a small spiral logo in the corner.

## Left side

* Toward the top: the USB-C port, with the charge light and the power button next to it.
* Nothing else is on the outside.

## Fit estimate

An estimate from the prototype parts, not a measurement:

* About 95 × 62 × 20 mm, and about 100–115 g.
* The Orange Pi Zero 2W (65 × 30 × 1.2 mm) and the HAT (65 × 30.2 mm) stack as one board block on the left, under the screen.
* The 1,800 mAh flat LiPo lies beside the board block on the right, under the wheel, so the two sit side by side instead of one on top of the other.
* The USB-C board edge faces the left side, behind the port. The charge light and the power button sit on the same side.
* A copper heatsink case won't fit inside, so it's a flat stick-on heatsink, kept away from the battery.
* The e-ink panel and the Back and Home key switches sit in the front half of the thickness. The wheel and its centre switch are on the right, over the battery.
